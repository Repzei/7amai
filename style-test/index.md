### 1. DeepSeek's new model codes for a tenth of what rivals charge {#deepseek-test}
<p class="item-meta"><span class="signal signal-big">Big deal</span><span class="track">Build</span><span class="track">1 min</span></p>

<p class="short"><span>The short version</span>A new open model from DeepSeek scores like the top models on a coding test, but each task costs cents instead of dollars.</p>

When you use an AI agent to write code, you do not pay for the answer. You pay for every word the model reads and writes on the way there. So the real price is the cost of finishing a task.

That is where DeepSeek's new Flash model stands out. On DeepSeek's own coding test, a task costs just under $0.20, while rivals land at $2 or more.

There is a catch. DeepSeek ran the test itself, and nobody has checked it yet.

<div class="why"><b>Why it matters</b><p>Think of it like a taxi that now charges per trip instead of per minute, and the trip got ten times cheaper.</p></div>

<p class="todo"><b>Try it:</b> test it on one of your own tasks and compare the cost per finished task.</p>
