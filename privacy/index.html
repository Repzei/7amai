Privacy policy

7am AI collects as little as possible: your email address and the language you chose. Nothing else.

Who is responsible

Jesper Larsen is the data controller for 7am AI. Contact: privacy@7amai.com.

What we collect and why

  • Your email address and language (English or Danish), so we can send you the newsletter you asked for.
  • Your subscription status (subscribed or unsubscribed) and when it changed, so we only email people who want it.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)). We use double opt-in: you are only added to the list after you click the link in the confirmation email. If you never confirm, you are not added.

Who processes the data

  • Resend sends the emails and stores the subscriber list.
  • Cloudflare runs the small service that receives your sign-up and confirmation.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts delivers the fonts on this website, which means your browser contacts Google.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your email address with anyone else.
  • We have not enabled open tracking or click tracking in our emails.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models. The newsletter is written with AI, but your email address is never sent to an AI service.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe. When you unsubscribe, you stop receiving emails right away. If you also want your address deleted entirely, email privacy@7amai.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can withdraw your consent at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).
